Privacy Policy

Anatomy Atlas · Last updated: 18 October 2026

This policy explains how Anatomy Atlas handles information. The app provides anatomy education and general reference.

Information handled by the app

The app does not require an account and does not use advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, or an app server that receives your study data. Favorites, study notes, language choice, and some display preferences are stored locally on your device and are not sent to the developer. Do not enter patient names, identifiers, or other identifiable health information in notes. A backup you export is saved to the location you choose and is not uploaded to the developer by the app.

Internet and third-party services

The bundled atlas and models can be used offline. When you choose to open an external reference or the optional embedded model viewer (including the Sketchfab heart model), your device connects to that third-party service. It may process technical information such as your IP address, browser or device details, connection data, or cookies under its own privacy policy. The app opens these resources only after your selection; third-party processing is governed by that provider.

Storage and deletion

Local notes and preferences remain on your device. Delete them in the app or uninstall the app. Delete exported backups separately in the file manager. Depending on your Android or iOS backup settings, the operating system may include app data in its own backup. These backups are not sent to the developer, and uninstalling the app may not remove existing system or exported backups.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children and does not provide an account or child profile.

Support and contact

If you email atekin592@gmail.com, the email address and details you choose to send are used to answer your support or privacy request and may be stored by the email provider. You may ask for a support conversation to be deleted by writing to the same address; records that must be retained by law may be kept for the required period. Do not send patient information or identifiable health data.

You can also use GitHub support. GitHub issues may be public; do not include personal information.
