# Commercial-use rights review — OpenStax request submitted

**Status as of 2026-10-14:** the Dundee inner-ear and Lissie Cowley kidney meshes were replaced in the current model GLBs with procedural geometry; requests for those removed meshes are no longer needed unless a new version reintroduces them. A new blocker was identified: the app labels its OpenStax *Anatomy and Physiology 2e*-linked study notes CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. The commercial reuse request was submitted through OpenStax's official Content Reuse Request Form on 2026-10-14. OpenStax confirmed receipt and says review may take a couple of weeks. No commercial permission has been granted yet. The Z-Anatomy inquiry is still a lineage/attribution clarification, not a substitute for complying with its CC BY-SA terms.

The planned app is paid. Do not ship OpenStax-derived or OpenStax-licensed notes in that paid app without a written commercial grant, or remove and independently rewrite the affected notes from suitable sources. A request is not a license, and silence is not consent.

- **Rights holder:** OpenStax / Rice University.

- **Official license statement:** https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/preface
- **Commercial reuse request form:** https://openstax.org/r/content-reuse-request
- **Status:** submitted 2026-10-14 via the official Content Reuse Request Form. Receipt confirmed on the form's thank-you page; review pending. No commercial permission has been received or verified.
- **Fallback:** remove the OpenStax-linked notes and any material derived from that book, replace them with independently authored content based on sources licensed for commercial reuse, and update the in-app source and license disclosures.

The submitted request identifies the app notes as adapted/translated study-card content for paid iOS and Android distribution worldwide, and asks OpenStax to specify fees, attribution, and license conditions. The precise note inventory remains under audit; provide it if OpenStax requests it. Keep any approval in writing and do not treat receipt of the request as a grant.
